ClipFlow Studio

AI product clip generation and TikTok posting automation

ClipFlow Studio is a self-hosted app that turns product photos into short AI-generated video clips and, optionally, publishes them to the user's own TikTok account using TikTok's official Content Posting API.

  • Upload product photos
  • Generate an 8s AI video clip with Google's image and video models
  • Connect your own TikTok account via TikTok Login Kit
  • Optionally auto-publish generated clips on a schedule you control
