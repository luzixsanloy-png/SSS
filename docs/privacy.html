Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

ClipFlow Studio ("the App") is a self-hosted application. Each user runs their own instance on their own computer or server; there is no shared, central service operated by a third party.

1. What data the App stores

  • Product names, descriptions, and photos you upload.
  • Generated video clips and job status/history.
  • TikTok account tokens (access token, refresh token) obtained via TikTok's official OAuth2 Login Kit, used only to publish videos on your behalf via the TikTok Content Posting API.

All of this data is stored locally in a SQLite database and local files on the machine running the App. It is not transmitted to any server operated by the App's developer.

2. Third-party services

The App calls two third-party APIs directly from your own machine, using credentials you provide:

  • Google Gemini API — to generate/enhance images and video from your product photos.
  • TikTok API — to authenticate your TikTok account and publish videos you choose to post.

Data sent to these services is governed by Google's and TikTok's own privacy policies.

3. Data retention and deletion

Since all data is stored locally, you control retention. Deleting a product or job in the App removes its associated files and database records. Disconnecting a TikTok account removes its stored tokens.

4. Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the person operating this instance of the App.
